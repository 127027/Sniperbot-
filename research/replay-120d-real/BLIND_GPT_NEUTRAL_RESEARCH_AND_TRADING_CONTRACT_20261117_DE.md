## 3. GPT intelligence and dynamic cadence
GPT alone performs the strategic research and chooses BUY, HOLD, SELL, WATCH or NO_TRADE; the local bot is a technical executor/veto and logger. Each virtual day 1–24 actual independent full GPT model invocations are allowed and logged. GPT sets planned daily cadence 1/4/12/24 as-of, may revise at any hourly lightweight check (e.g. 4→24 after third full scan); max 24 actual full scans in 24 hours and min 1 hour apart. A workflow that loops a fixed threshold 513 times is a research *proxy*, NOT 513 model invocations.

Each full GPT review first inspects all previously established open positions, available bidirectional executable prices/LP/fee/slippage, and any risk or liquidity changes. It separately examines all neutral new candidates, media origin and contemporaneous onchain behavior, chooses an action and a justification; a high score or a viral post is NOT a BUY command without independent sellability and costed quote. Sell is allowed to cut a confirmed loss (not guaranteed always profitable). Missed winners are audited as carefully as poor buys.
