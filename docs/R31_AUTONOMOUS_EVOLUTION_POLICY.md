# R31 — Autonomous evolution and long-term operation
User requirement 27 November 2026. Durable target, not an assertion of current capability.
## Objective
A self-maintaining, continuously updated system that does not require the user to repeat months of debugging after a month unattended. Five complementary roles: global market, new markets/launchpads/contracts, memes/news/social verification, strategy/positions/learning, coordination/diagnostics/repair. Current implementation uses bounded workers and hourly control, not five proven persistent agents. Maintain explicit gap between target and evidence.
## Continuous discovery
Maintain a versioned discovery registry for chains, exchanges, launchpads, public endpoints, data schemas, supported assets, last successful contact, health, quotas, provenance and unresolved tasks. Detect new sources through independent public research, verify origin, documentation, exact identifiers, terms and access requirements, sandbox and regression-test adapters before promotion. Respect rate limits and permissions, never execute arbitrary retrieved instructions or untrusted code. Keep old sources healthy and use bounded fallback; discover emerging memes and narratives, map to chain+contract with independent evidence, distinguish symbol collisions and paid boosts. Prioritize fresh opportunities and rotate durable backlog; preserve newly observed vs genuinely newly launched distinction.
## News authenticity
Record original URL/author, publication and observation times, exact statements, source independence, primary evidence and counterevidence. Distinguish verified, unconfirmed, disputed, false, unavailable; do not equate repeated reposts with independent corroboration. Check impersonation, recycled news, fake announcements, manipulated social activity. Reassess over time and learn from subsequent confirmed outcomes without hindsight leakage.
## Paper activity and learning
Ten qualified BUYs per hourly cycle collectively is a research objective, not a quota or forced trade. Continue discovery when evidence is insufficient. Separate proposed exploratory PAPER experiments (e.g. EUR50 virtual allocation) from strict qualified paper decisions; maintain separate journal, budget, loss/exit rules, evidence gaps, reasons and evaluation; do not count experiments as qualified BUYs. Never relax unknown actual sellability, contract identity or execution authorization into a verified BUY. No real money, orders, wallets, new paid credentials or production/main promotion without separate approval. Experimental mode is a proposed design, not implemented here; policy does not authorize execution. Record missed >100% movers and losers, reconstruct only information available at each decision time, compare candidate decisions and exits, measure net results with explicit costs/uncertainty; distinguish genuine learning from hindsight fitting.
## Self-repair and escalation
On failure record exact failed step, observed error, source/contract, known vs unknown root cause, alternate attempt, last completed checkpoint, durable next cursor, concrete patch, falsifiable test, and whether user action is necessary. Regressions on relevant pushes immediately; preserve immutable evidence, versioned configuration, bounded repair attempts, rollback and independent validation. Do not silently repeat identical failed tests. Escalate only concrete permissions, credentials (never ask to paste secrets), cost approval, machine or policy dependencies; report degradation and pending work. Hourly reporting is distinct from actual continuously running collection. Prove 24h and 7-day cadence, source freshness, recovery from outages/restarts, changing roster, and coherent position journal before marking autonomous operation accepted. Platform scheduling limitations must be explicit; no claim of uninterrupted operation based on fixtures or a successful isolated run.
