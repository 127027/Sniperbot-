# R31 — Market-wide top ten

MARKET-WIDE TOP 10 — binding clarification approved 28 Nov 2026. Ten is solely the output limit, never the discovery/deep research stopping criterion. Compare the widest reachable cross-chain market, fresh launches, existing candidates, support leads and news/social catalysts, including coins not in registry. Continue evaluating and replace weaker candidates whenever stronger ones appear. Rank forward-looking risk-adjusted opportunity, not historical percentage gains. Keep full candidate backlog and explicit exclusions, source/chain coverage, scanned/matched/evaluated counts, freshness, unresolved identities, and explain why selected candidates beat alternatives. If coverage incomplete mark PARTIAL; never call a small sample global top ten. Separate ranked speculative watchlist from qualified hypothetical BUYs; fewer than ten if evidence insufficient, no padding, no forced buys. Preserve all safety constraints, source timestamps, €50 hypothetical allocation and €5000 comparison budget. Review existing positions independently. Continue useful work beyond ten until actual invocation limits, persist cursor. Compare the independently verified 12h winners to contemporaneous recommendations, not hindsight. This rule supersedes older wording that implies stop after ten.

## Acceptance
- Regression: present eleven or more eligible candidates with a stronger late arrival; it must displace the weakest, independent of input order.
- Report actual market coverage and remaining backlog; distinguish incomplete research from completed ranking.
- Track recommendation recall, actual paper fills, losses and costs separately.
- No change to schedules, production or live execution.
